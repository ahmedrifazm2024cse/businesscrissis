# 🏆 Agentverse Judge Demo Guide

This guide is designed for a 5-minute hackathon pitch to flawlessly demonstrate the Autonomous Business Commander AI.

## The 5-Minute Flow

### 0:00 - 1:00: The Problem & The Architecture
- **Action**: Open the `Home Dashboard`.
- **Talk Track**: "Modern enterprises are too slow to react to multi-faceted crises. We built the Agentverse: an army of 13 specialized AI agents, orchestrated by a central Executive Commander, that can diagnose, plan, and mitigate enterprise disasters in milliseconds."

### 1:00 - 2:00: Launching the Crisis
- **Action**: Navigate to `Presentation Mode` (`/presentation` in the sidebar).
- **Action**: Click "Launch Crisis" on the **Combined Multi-Crisis Event**.
- **Talk Track**: "Let's simulate a nightmare scenario: A cyber attack leaking credit cards, tanking our stock, and causing a PR meltdown. Watch the system react."

### 2:00 - 3:30: The Live Workflow Monitor
- **Action**: The UI will auto-redirect to the `Workflow Monitor`.
- **Talk Track**: "This is our Live DAG. The Executive Commander has parsed the crisis and dynamically spun up the Workflow Manager. You can see the Cyber Agent, PR Agent, and Finance Agent being triggered simultaneously. The blue pulsing nodes mean they are actively communicating over our real-time EventBus."

### 3:30 - 4:30: Executive Decision & Output
- **Action**: Switch to the `Agent Monitor` tab to show the raw JSON/WebSocket stream.
- **Talk Track**: "Notice the speed. 13 different LLMs are working in parallel. The Decision Agent is now aggregating their mitigations, and the Report Generator is finalizing the response."

### 4:30 - 5:00: The Close
- **Action**: Show the final Executive Report (Output JSON).
- **Talk Track**: "In under 30 seconds, our AI platform neutralized a company-ending crisis. It’s fully Dockerized, Kubernetes ready, and built on Enterprise Solid architecture. Thank you."
